> **North star:** a Persian speaker can do everything in a terminal that an English speaker can (**read, write, edit, search, select, copy and paste**) with no workarounds, in any terminal, multiplexer, editor or command-line app, on any operating system.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Shaping" icon="puzzle">
    A letter's form depends on its neighbours. A grid of independent cells draws them disconnected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bidi" icon="random">
    Visual order depends on the whole paragraph (UAX #9). Words come out reversed, numbers misplaced, brackets unmirrored.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Width" icon="seti:grain">
    لا is two characters but one glyph, and ZWNJ has no width. The app and the terminal disagree about where the cursor is.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ownership" icon="warning">
    Nobody has settled whether the terminal or the app owns bidi. If both do it, the text is reversed twice.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
