# Code of Conduct

persian-cli adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming, be patient with people working in their second (or third) language, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in any language or script.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, Discussions, the website, and any channel that represents the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainer, [@mbaneshi](https://github.com/mbaneshi), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer will review every report and respond as the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) describe: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban.
