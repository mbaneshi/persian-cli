The research program is proposed in [RFC 0001](https://github.com/mbaneshi/persian-cli/discussions/12) and is open for comment. Every phase is a [milestone](https://github.com/mbaneshi/persian-cli/milestones); every item is on the [roadmap board](https://github.com/users/mbaneshi/projects/22).

<Steps>

1. **Phase 0: Root-cause map** <Badge text="In progress" variant="caution" />

   Primary-source notes on who owns what, layer by layer. The [prior-work survey](/persian-cli/prior-work/) is done.
   *Exit:* every layer has a written root-cause note with sources.

2. **Phase 1: Test corpus & scorecard** <Badge text="Next" variant="note" />

   Hard test strings, plus a harness that scores any terminal + multiplexer + app stack.
   *Exit:* a v0 scorecard published for at least 3 terminals.

3. **Phase 2: Fonts & terminals** <Badge text="Planned" />

   Font recommendations, configurations and upstream rendering fixes.

4. **Phase 3: Multiplexers** <Badge text="Planned" />

   Shaping and bidi passed through tmux and Zellij intact.

5. **Phase 4: Editors & TUI frameworks** <Badge text="Planned" />

   Fixes or plugins for Neovim, readline/zle, Ink, ratatui and similar.

6. **Phase 5: Bidi ownership standard** <Badge text="Planned" />

   Working with maintainers to finish and adopt a bidi negotiation standard.

</Steps>

## Current focus

The three gaps the [prior-work survey](/persian-cli/prior-work/) found that nobody has touched, plus the corpus every fix will be measured against.
